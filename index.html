How to use Curvewright ▸
Enter or paste data into the table on the left. Column names in formulas use the short name shown under each header (e.g. t, x).
Calculated columns can use a formula (v = dx/dt style expressions, e.g. x^2 + 3*sin(t)), or be a numeric derivative or integral of two existing columns.
Graphs pick X and one or more Y columns. Use the region boxes (or the "Pick start/end" buttons, tap a point on the chart) to restrict statistics and curve fits to part of the data.
FFT — switch a graph's view to "Spectrum (FFT)" to see the frequency content of a Y column against evenly-spaced X data. Use "Send peak to fit" to seed a Sinusoidal fit with the dominant frequency.
Saving your work — your table and graphs autosave in this browser. To keep data permanently, use Export CSV (copies to clipboard) or "Save as image" on a graph (press-and-hold the image to save it to Photos on iPad).
